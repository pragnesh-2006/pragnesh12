How EssayLens works

This project interface demonstrates an automated essay scoring workflow. A production system can preprocess an essay, extract linguistic, structural, and semantic signals, and pass them to a trained scoring model.

01Prepare the textNormalize text and identify words, sentences, and paragraphs.
02Analyze writingMeasure organization, language variety, clarity, mechanics, and meaning.
03Train and compareCompare classical models and transformer approaches using QWK, MAE, and correlation.
04Predict and explainReturn a consistent score with learning-focused feedback for revision.

Scores shown here are illustrative demo estimates. They are not produced by a trained or validated model. The interface is designed to connect to a validated model, including BERT-based scoring.
